# Field Guides


## Field notes
| Field | Common designs | Evidence standards and resources |
|---|---|---|
| Education | RCTs, quasi-experiments, classroom studies | What Works Clearinghouse standards, EEF toolkit, effect sizes in SD units |
| Psychology | Experiments, surveys, longitudinal studies | Pre-registration, replication (watch for the replication crisis), large samples |
| Business and management | Surveys, field experiments, case studies | Meta-analyses, top journals, caution with self-reported data |
| Economics | Natural experiments, difference-in-differences, regression discontinuity | Working papers (NBER), robustness checks |
| Law | Case law, statutes, legal scholarship, empirical legal studies | Primary sources first; jurisdiction and date matter |
| Environment and climate | Observations, models, meta-analyses | IPCC assessment reports as the main synthesis |
| Engineering and computing | Experiments, benchmarks, simulations | Reproducible code and data, peer-reviewed conferences |
| Social sciences | Surveys, ethnography, mixed methods | Sampling quality, context, theory |
| Humanities | Close reading, archival research | Primary sources, scholarly editions, peer-reviewed monographs |

## How to use
Identify the field, apply its standards when appraising studies, and search its main databases (see database-guide). For topics that cross fields, search each field's sources.
