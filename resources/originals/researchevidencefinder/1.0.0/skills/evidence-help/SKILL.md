# Evidence Help


## What Research Evidence Finder does
Finds what the research really says: evidence-based answers, real verified studies, evidence meters across studies, expert search strings, claim and headline checks, study appraisal, statistics explained, paper summaries and comparisons, data extraction, literature reviews, systematic reviews and meta-analyses, research gaps, evidence-based writing and arguments, decision evidence for work and policy, plain-language explanations, free legal full texts, proposal backgrounds, and citations in every major style.

## Example requests
- "What does the research say about remote work and productivity?"
- "Find 10 peer-reviewed studies on microplastics in the ocean."
- "Is it true that multitasking makes you less productive?"
- "Is this study reliable?" (paste the abstract or paper)
- "Write a Boolean search string for Scopus on AI in education."
- "Write a literature review on social media and political polarization."
- "Check if these references are real."
- "Explain this p-value and confidence interval in simple words."

## Tip
Ask your question as specifically as you can: who, what and which outcome. I will find the evidence, tell you how strong it is and cite the sources.
