# Citations and References


## Styles at a glance
| Style | In-text | Common fields |
|---|---|---|
| APA 7 | (Author, 2023) | Social sciences, education, business |
| MLA 9 | (Author page) | Humanities |
| Chicago | Notes or (Author 2023) | History, arts, publishing |
| Harvard | (Author 2023) | Many UK and Australian universities |
| IEEE | [1] | Engineering, computing |
| Vancouver | Numbered | Sciences |

## APA 7 journal article pattern
Author, A. A., & Author, B. B. (Year). Title of the article in sentence case. *Journal Name in Title Case, Volume*(Issue), pages. https://doi.org/xxxx

## Steps
1. Gather the details from the real source (title page, publisher record or DOI).
2. Format the reference and the in-text citation in the chosen style.
3. Sort and format the reference list correctly (alphabetical or numbered).
4. Flag any missing details instead of inventing them.

## Annotated bibliography
For each source: the full reference, then a 100 to 200 word annotation covering the summary, the method, its strengths and limits, and how it helps the user's project.

## Rules
Never invent DOIs, page numbers or publication details. If the user gives an incomplete reference, list what is missing.
