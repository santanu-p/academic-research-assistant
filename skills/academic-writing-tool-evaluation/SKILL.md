# Evaluate academic writing tools

Identify the user's task, target environment, budget, language, collaboration needs, accessibility requirements and institutional policies. Distinguish a local editing workflow from a hosted service: uploading a manuscript, reviewer correspondence or research data may disclose confidential material. Do not create accounts, submit text, start a trial or purchase a subscription without the user's authorization.

Build a short comparison based on the capabilities relevant to the task. Check current primary documentation for platform support, supported file formats, pricing, usage limits, data retention and training terms, institutional access and applicable disclosure rules. Record the date and source for consequential claims; vendor descriptions are claims about features, not independent proof of quality or privacy. When the user has not supplied private text, use a synthetic or already authorized sample for any trial.

For example, [Paperpal's help center](https://support.paperpal.com/support/solutions/articles/3000126445-what-all-can-paperpal-be-used-for-) describes language editing, paraphrasing and reference-related features; check the current product documentation and plan before recommending a particular feature. Consider other available tools or a local workflow when they fit the requirements. Do not assume that a provider's claims, a tool's availability on a particular platform, or an advertised free tier remain unchanged.

Evaluate suggestions against the actual source: preserve technical meaning, numbers, equations, references and uncertainty. Verify cited works and claims independently; a grammar checker, similarity report or AI detector is not proof of correctness, plagiarism, authorship or compliance. Present strengths, relevant limitations and costs as verified or unknown. Disclose a real affiliation only if it is established; do not fabricate affiliate links or imply endorsement.

Deliver a concise comparison and a defensible recommendation tied to the user's constraints. If the needed pricing, privacy terms or hands-on test are unavailable, state what was not verified and what the user would need to check before relying on the tool.
