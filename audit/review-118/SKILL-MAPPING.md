## Why two pairs were merged

**Research gaps:** both analyze the same literature base using the same seven gap types and turn gaps into questions/designs. The combined method keeps discovery and recency checks, population/setting/method/outcome mapping, source/page evidence, importance, 3–6 candidate gaps, and separate gap/contribution paragraphs. Corpus-limited observations remain provisional.

**Systematic reviews:** both cover the same reproducible-review lifecycle. The combined method keeps every framework, review-type choice, eligibility dimension, search/screening/extraction method, disagreement rule, bias tool, synthesis option, certainty rating, flow count and protocol/extraction/PRISMA template. Actual execution remains distinct from planning.

**Deliberate retentions:** evidence answers retain retrieval/verification commitments; scholar synthesis supports a bounded supplied corpus. Evidence literature review retains discovery/screening/review-type selection; scholar literature review keeps source-bound matrices and its independent annotated-bibliography mode. Shared substeps are documented without erasing these whole-skill differences.
