# Plan and fund research

Inputs: brief, sponsor/call or institution requirements, actual team resources, timeline, budget evidence and existing contributions.

1. Use `scholar-research-proposal`, `evidence-research-proposal-background` and the discovery workflow for a bounded rationale and feasible objectives.
2. Read `scholar-grant-proposal`; align aims, methods, milestones, alternatives and evaluation with the actual call. Verify current call requirements when available; mark inaccessible requirements unverified.
3. Use `academic-humanizer` for proposal claims matched to feasibility and author voice. Preserve proposed versus completed work, collaborator roles and supplied numbers. Do not invent preliminary results, facilities, approvals or budget figures.
4. Use `scholar-research-planner` for milestones, dependencies, writing schedule and meeting plans; include risks and alternative paths.

Outputs: complete requested proposal/grant sections, work plan, evidence-backed resource/budget notes and outstanding requirements. Gate: each aim has a feasible test and resources; current requirements are verified or visibly unresolved.
